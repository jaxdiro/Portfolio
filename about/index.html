About

Hi, I'm Jackie Diaz — Designer, Marketer &
Biker.

I'm Jackie Diaz, a marketing and communications coordinator based in Nelson, BC. I lead integrated marketing at Kootenay Co-op — a member-owned grocery cooperative serving the West Kootenays — where I own the strategy, creative direction, and paid media behind every campaign.

Outside of that, I run a freelance communications-design practice and take on volunteer marketing leadership and content creation for Building Momentum Bikepacking, an inclusive bikepacking nonprofit. Across all three, the throughline is the same: turn one person's worth of marketing into a system that scales past just posting content.

How I Work

Three parts, one connected system.

Every project I take on gets broken into the same three buckets — so strategy, creative, and paid media are always pulling toward the same goal instead of competing for attention.

Strategy & Planning
Setting the calendar, the goals, and the reporting framework everything else runs on.
Creative Direction & Production
Directing and producing the content itself — photography, copy, campaign identity.
Paid Media & Analytics
Running the paid social program and closing the loop with reporting that actually gets used.

Why bikepacking keeps showing up

A lot of my personal time goes toward bikepacking — planning routes, riding them, and volunteering for Building Momentum Bikepacking. It's not unrelated to the work: both come down to building a system other people can rely on, whether that's a campaign calendar or a resupply plan.
