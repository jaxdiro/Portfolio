FlowStep lays out a repeatable system as a row of steps joined by arrows.

- Provide an index word (Before, During, After, Between, or Week 5), a one-word action label, and one sentence of detail.
- Steps are `panel` boxes with a `line` border and `radius-sm`. Connectors are `violet` arrows; on phones the row wraps and the arrows turn to point down.
- Use four to five steps. More than five, split into two rows.
- Fade the steps in left to right once when the section scrolls into view, 140ms apart; under reduced motion show them at once.
