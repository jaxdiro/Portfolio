A 5-week integrated campaign run across web, email, social, and in-store channels — using Member Appreciation Day and the Case Lot Sale as the example, walked through from planning through to results.

The Approach

Strategy & Planning

Built a five-week phased rollout — planning and asset development, then a staged awareness build, then launch — so every channel moved on the same clock instead of its own schedule.

Creative Direction & Production

Directed and produced every creative asset — static and carousel posts, short- and long-form video, website banners, print ads, and in-store collateral — holding one consistent visual approach across all of it.

Paid Media & Analytics

Staged Meta and Google ad spend to match the awareness curve — early ads introducing the campaign, then countdown messaging as the sale approached — with performance reporting across the full run.
