Building Momentum Bikepacking · Volunteer Marketing Leadership

A zero-budget content system that reached 22x its own audience.

Building Momentum Bikepacking is a nonprofit creating inclusive bikepacking spaces for women, non-binary, and gender non-conforming riders through workshops, rides, and educational programming. As Marketing & Digital Engagement Lead, I built and ran its organic communications system — content strategy, photography and video, and event promotion — with no advertising budget behind it.

The Approach

Strategy & Planning

Content strategy shaped by what the engagement data actually showed — platform-native formats, a consistent posting rhythm, and analytics reviewed regularly to steer direction rather than guess at it.

Creative Direction & Production

Photography, videography, and short-form video editing built around real participants and real workshop moments — the storytelling that consistently outperformed direct promotional posts.

Community & Event Engagement

Pre-event promotion, live documentation, and post-event recap content that kept the community connected in the gaps between workshops, rides, and campaigns.

The Content System

Every event ran through the same loop — not a one-off push, but a repeatable system that kept working between events too.

Before
Announce
Pre-event promotional content across posts, stories, and reels on Instagram and Facebook.
→
During
Document
Live photo and video capture at workshops and rides — the raw material for everything after.
→
After
Recap
Post-event recap content, cross-posted across socials and event pages, adapted into short-form video.
→
Between
Sustain
Participant-centered storytelling that kept the community engaged until the next event's cycle began.

The Results

120.3K
Organic views — roughly 153x the combined audience size
17.6K
Accounts reached organically — roughly 22x the follower count
$0
Paid advertising spend behind any of it

Built from a combined base of 785 followers across Instagram and Facebook, across 58 published content pieces over twelve months. The system also drove real-world participation: a fundraiser event sold out with 130 responses, and a second workshop was added mid-year after the first sold out on demand.
