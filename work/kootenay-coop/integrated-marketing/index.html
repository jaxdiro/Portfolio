Kootenay Co-op · Integrated Marketing Strategy

A five-week system behind one of Kootenay Co-op's biggest sales of the year.

For Member Appreciation Day and the Case Lot Sale — two of the co-op's largest annual promotions — I built and ran an integrated communications campaign that coordinated paid, owned, and in-store channels into a single phased rollout, from the first announcement through the final day of the sale.

Kootenay Co-op Marketing & Communications Coordinator 5-week phased rollout

The Challenge

Member Appreciation Day and the Case Lot Sale are among Kootenay Co-op's largest annual promotions — and among the most channel-heavy. A single campaign needed to reach members through digital advertising, the website, email, print, and in-store signage, all saying the same thing at the same time, from the first announcement through the final day of the sale.

That meant treating it as one campaign instead of five separate pushes: a single strategy and creative direction driving paid ads, owned channels, and in-store promotion together, with a phased timeline that built awareness deliberately instead of announcing everything at once.

The Approach

Strategy & Planning

Built a five-week phased rollout — planning and asset development, then a staged awareness build, then launch — so every channel moved on the same clock instead of its own schedule.

Creative Direction & Production

Directed and produced every creative asset in the campaign — static and carousel social posts, short- and long-form video, website and e-commerce banners, print ads, and in-store collateral — introducing carousel-style posts specifically to give the storytelling more room, and holding one consistent visual approach across all of it.

Paid Media & Analytics

Staged Meta and Google ad spend to match the awareness curve — early ads introducing the campaign, then a second phase layering in countdown messaging as the sale approached — and closed the loop with performance reporting across the full run.
