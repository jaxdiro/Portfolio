Kootenay Co-op · Content Creation

The everyday content that keeps four channels alive.

Beyond any single campaign, I'm the lead content creator for Kootenay Co-op — planning, shooting, and designing the photography, video, and graphics that fill the website, email, social, and in-store channels every week, with one consistent visual approach across all of it.

Kootenay Co-op Marketing & Communications Coordinator Ongoing
Role
Photography, videography, graphic design, copywriting
Organization
Member-owned grocery co-op, West Kootenays
Focus
Static & carousel posts · short & long-form video · website & print design

What I Create

Every channel — website, email, social, and in-store — runs on the same content pipeline. I plan the shot list, shoot and design the graphics, and produce the video, then hold one consistent visual language across all of it so a social post, a website banner, and a printed flyer all read as the same brand.

Carousel-style posts were my own addition to the format mix, introduced specifically to give the content more room for storytelling alongside the static graphics, short-form promotional video, and long-form video that round out the rest of the library.
