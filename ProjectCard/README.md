ProjectCard is one entry in the Work grid: name, kind, type tag, a practice-area list that opens on hover, a link, and an image slot.

- Provide the project name, one-line kind, a Tag, the practice areas, the link text, and a 1020 × 1350 image.
- Rest state is `card` with `ink` and `ink-soft` text. Hover and keyboard focus fill the card `lavender`, switch all text to `on-lavender` and lift it 2px with `shadow-lift`. The practice areas slide open at the same time.
- The whole card is one link. The spinning star sits before the link text; it turns `on-lavender` on hover.
- Grid: three columns on desktop, one on phones, `space-5` gap. Do not put two cards of different heights in one row.
- Until real work images exist, keep the dashed `ds-ph` box with its pixel size.
